*A label is a starting point for your own research, not a guarantee.*

On the DeFi section of our home page, each kind of app carries a risk label. Here is how to read them.

## What the labels mean

- **Lower**: fewer moving parts, and the main risks are well understood. It can still lose money.
- **Medium**: several kinds of risk can combine. For example, a smart contract bug plus a price move that triggers liquidation.
- **High**: the app depends on things that can change fast, such as temporary reward rates, new tokens, or a stable asset that could lose its peg.

No label means a DeFi app is "safe". We use the words Lower, Medium and High on purpose.

## The risks behind the labels

- **Smart contract risk**: code can have bugs, even after an audit.
- **Liquidation risk**: if you borrow, a drop in your collateral's price can force a sale.
- **Impermanent loss**: providing liquidity can leave you with less value than just holding.
- **Depeg risk**: a stablecoin can trade below its intended price.
- **Scam risk**: fake apps and copycat tokens look real. Always check you are on the official site.

## What a label cannot tell you

A label cannot see who runs an app today, whether its code changed yesterday, or whether the rate you see will last. Check these yourself before you deposit.

## A simple checklist

1. Can you explain in one sentence how the app earns its return?
2. Do you know how to withdraw, and how long it takes?
3. Have you practiced the whole flow on a testnet first?
4. Would you be fine losing the amount you plan to deposit?

If you answer no to any of these, wait. Read our guide to [DeFi yield risks](/blog/defi-yield-risks/) for more detail.
