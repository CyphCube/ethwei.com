*Practice first. Mistakes on a testnet cost nothing.*

DeFi can feel intimidating. Wallets, gas fees, approvals and swaps all use new words, and a wrong click on a real network can cost real money. A **testnet** removes that risk. It works like the real network, but its tokens are free and have no value.

## Step 1: Set up a wallet safely

Install your wallet only from its official website or app store page. When it shows you a **recovery phrase**, write it on paper and keep it somewhere private. Never type it into a website, send it in a message, or store it in a photo or cloud note. No genuine support team will ever ask for it.

## Step 2: Add a test network

Most wallets let you switch to a test network. Pick one, and the wallet will show a separate balance for it. Nothing on a test network touches your real funds.

## Step 3: Get free test tokens

Use an official faucet to request test tokens. For test USDC, use the faucet from Circle. You will usually need to paste your wallet address and wait a moment for the tokens to arrive.

## Step 4: Make a small test transaction

Send a small amount of test tokens to a second wallet you control. Watch the wallet show the transaction as pending, then confirmed. Every transaction pays a small **gas fee**, which you can compare on the Gas section of the home page.

## Step 5: Read what happened

Copy the transaction hash and open it in a block explorer for that test network. You will see who sent what, when, and how much gas it used. Learning to read an explorer is one of the most useful DeFi skills.

## Before you move to real money

- Repeat the steps until each one feels routine.
- Read how each app can fail. See our guide to [DeFi yield risks](/blog/defi-yield-risks/).
- Start with a small amount you can afford to lose.
- Be careful of anyone who promises guaranteed returns.
